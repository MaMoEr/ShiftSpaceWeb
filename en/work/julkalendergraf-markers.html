Instructions

How to use the marker

1. Print the marker

  • Print the image at actual size (100%), so it is 20 cm wide. Measure it to check – the wrong size places the scene wrongly in the room.
  • Use plain white paper, preferably matte. Glossy paper can reflect light and make the image hard for the camera to see.

2. Put it on the wall

  • Fix the marker to a wall with the centre of the image 1.5 metres above the floor.
  • Keep it straight, smooth and flat against the wall – no folds or bubbles. Taping all four corners works well.
  • Choose a wall with free floor space in front of it, a few metres. The calendar's scenes appear in the room in front of the wall.

Tips

  • Use good, even lighting. Avoid sunlight or a lamp shining directly on the marker.
  • Move the phone calmly. If the scene ends up crooked, restart the app and point at the marker again.
  • It helps if the floor is visible to the camera – the app uses it to place the scene at the right height.
  • Stay aware of your surroundings while looking through the camera, and let children use the app together with an adult.
