HÄR-torget

About the project

On the main square in Härnösand stand a few colourful shopfront sets where children and adults play sweet shop, shoe shop, bank, or hot dog stand.

When the play winds down, you can carry on with your phone. One experience for each shop.

In the Hot dog stand you can eat a hot dog, but watch out for the toppings!
In the Shoe shop you can put your foot in the shoe, by singing!?
In the Sweet shop you can of course eat sweets, as many as you like.
